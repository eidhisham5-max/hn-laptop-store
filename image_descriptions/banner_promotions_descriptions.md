# Banner Promotions Image Descriptions

## Option 1: Black Friday Sale Banner
**Scene Description:**
A dynamic, eye-catching banner featuring a Black Friday sale promotion. The banner shows a laptop with a prominent discount percentage (e.g., "50% OFF") in large, bold typography. The background features a dark, high-contrast design with orange and black colors. The laptop is positioned at an angle with dramatic lighting to create excitement. The banner includes countdown timer elements and call-to-action buttons.

**Key Elements:**
- Laptop product image (MacBook Pro or Dell XPS)
- Large discount percentage ("50% OFF")
- Dark background with orange accents
- Countdown timer elements
- Call-to-action buttons
- Bold, attention-grabbing typography

**Technical Specifications:**
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (1920x1080)
- Style: Dynamic promotional design
- Lighting: Dramatic, high-contrast lighting
- Color Temperature: Cool (6000K-7000K)
- Focus: Sharp focus on laptop and text

**Emotional Tone:**
Exciting, urgent, exclusive, high-value, action-oriented

---

## Option 2: New Arrivals Banner
**Scene Description:**
A clean, modern banner showcasing new laptop arrivals. The banner features multiple laptop models arranged in an elegant layout with soft, professional lighting. The background is light and airy with subtle gradients. The text "New Arrivals" is prominently displayed with clean typography. The banner includes subtle animation elements and a "Shop Now" call-to-action button.

**Key Elements:**
- Multiple laptop models (MacBook, Dell XPS, HP Pavilion)
- Clean, modern layout
- Light background with subtle gradients
- "New Arrivals" text
- "Shop Now" call-to-action button
- Elegant, professional typography

**Technical Specifications:**
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (1920x1080)
- Style: Clean, modern promotional design
- Lighting: Soft, professional lighting
- Color Temperature: Neutral (5500K-6000K)
- Focus: Sharp focus on all laptops

**Emotional Tone:**
Fresh, modern, exciting, new, aspirational

## Usage Guidelines
- **Option 1** is best for time-sensitive promotions and sales
- **Option 2** is ideal for new product launches and arrivals
- **Option 3** works well for service promotions and benefits
- All options should be available in both Arabic and English versions
- Consider creating variations with different laptop models and brands
- Ensure banners are optimized for web use (WebP format, responsive sizes)
- Maintain consistent branding and color schemes across all banners
