## Usage Guidelines
- **Option 1** is best for empty cart states and encouraging shopping
- **Option 2** is ideal for cart with items and product information
- **Option 3** works well for checkout process and payment flow
- All options should be available in both Arabic and English versions
- Consider creating variations with different laptop models and brands
- Ensure images are optimized for web use (WebP format, responsive sizes)
- Maintain consistent branding and color schemes across all cart page images
